# Website administrator quick guide

Use the admin dashboard to update the Mozambique Renewable Energy Venture website, track enquiries and respond to whistleblowing reports.

## Sign in and manage access

- Open `/admin/login.html` on the website. Enter your administrator email and password, then select **Sign in**. Use the sidebar or the dashboard cards to open a section.
- If you forget your password, select **Forgot password?**, enter your admin email and select **Send reset link**. Open the link in your email to set a new password.
- To update your password while signed in, open **Change password** and enter your current password and the new password twice.
- To give someone their own account, open **Add administrator**, enter their email and initial password, confirm the password and select **Create administrator**. The initial password needs at least 12 characters, including uppercase and lowercase letters and a number. Ask the new administrator to change it after signing in.
- Select **Log out** when you finish.

## Update website content

For **Articles**, **Products**, **Team** and **FAQs**, choose **English** or **Portuguese** before editing. Make the corresponding update in each language where it should appear. Articles, products and team profiles set to **Draft** stay out of public listings. Choose **Published** and save when they are ready. FAQ changes are published when saved.

| Section | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Company settings** | Update the company name, motto, logo, address, phone, WhatsApp or social links. Include country codes for phone and WhatsApp numbers. Leave **Map location** blank to use the physical address. To replace the logo, enter its URL or choose a file and select **Upload logo**. Select **Save company settings**. |
| **Articles** | Open the new article editor or select a saved article. Enter the title, slug, optional summary and article body. The slug is the URL identifier and fills in from the title for new articles. Choose a picture or enter its URL, set the status and select **Save article**. Keep the existing slug if the article is already shared. |
| **Products** | Open the new product editor or select a saved product. Enter the name and description, choose availability and currency, and enter the price in **New price**. Use **Old price** when showing a previous price. Use **Add property** for details such as capacity. Choose an image or enter its URL, set the status and select **Save product**. |
| **Team** | Select the language and use **Save introduction** to update the team and culture text. Use **New member** or **Edit** to update a profile. Enter the name, role and summary, and optionally enter a portrait URL or use **Upload portrait**. Choose the status and select **Save member**. Use **Move up** or **Move down** to change the profile order. |
| **Partner logos** | Select **New partner** or **Edit**. Enter the partner name and website URL. Supply a logo URL or choose a file and select **Upload logo**, then select **Save partner**. Use **Move up** or **Move down** to change the carousel order. |
| **FAQs** | Choose the language, then select **New FAQ** or **Edit** beside an existing entry. Enter the question and answer and select **Save FAQ**. To remove an entry, use **Delete** and confirm **Delete FAQ** in the dialog. |

For article and product images, choosing a file starts the upload. Wait for it to finish before saving. After publishing, open the affected page on the public website and check the language, text, images and any links you changed.

## Review queries and leads

1. Open **Queries and leads** and find the submission under **Submitted queries**.
2. Select **View more** to read the form responses, contact details and consent information.
3. Use the **Query status** dropdown to mark the submission **New**, **In progress** or **Resolved**. The change saves automatically. Look for **Saved** beside the dropdown.
4. To change where query notifications go, enter the new address in **Recipient email** and select **Save email setting**.

## Respond to whistleblowing reports

1. Open **Whistleblowing** and review the report details and **Conversation**. Keep report details within the authorized case-handling process.
2. Type your reply in the response box and select **Send response**. The reply appears in the conversation as **Organization** and is visible to the reporter.
3. Select **Close issue** when follow-up is complete. If the conversation needs to continue, select **Reopen issue** to return it to **In progress** and enable replies.
