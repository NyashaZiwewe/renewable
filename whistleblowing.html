Confidential reporting channel

Whistleblowing

This channel allows you to report concerns about suspected wrongdoing connected with the organization. You can submit a new report or continue a conversation using a follow-up token.

Share what happened, who was involved, and when it took place. Include links to relevant evidence if you have them. You may submit a report without providing an email address; keep your follow-up token safe if you want to return to the conversation.
