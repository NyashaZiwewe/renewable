Biofertilizer for circular agriculture

Digestate generated by biodigesters can support a circular agriculture approach when handled, stored and applied correctly.

Digestate pathway

  1. Separation or storage according to technical guidance
  2. Application in doses suitable for soil and crop
  3. Monitoring crop response
  4. Recording practices for learning and improvement

Possible benefits

Productive use of waste

Support for soil fertility

Reduced organic waste

Integration between energy and agriculture

Yield, productivity or savings guarantees should not be published. Results depend on soil, crop, climate, dosage and farm management.
