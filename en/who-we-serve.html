Solutions adapted to the user

Each segment has different needs, risks and feasibility criteria.

Rural and peri-urban households

Needs: Lighting, charging, clean cooking and simple support.

Solutions: Solar home systems and biogas where sufficient feedstock is available.

Farmers and livestock enterprises

Needs: Waste management, thermal energy and improved agricultural practices.

Solutions: Biodigesters, biofertilizer and operating monitoring.

Schools and community facilities

Needs: Safe solutions, training, maintenance and accountability.

Solutions: Institutional projects with assessment, installation and follow-up.

NGOs, businesses and partners

Needs: Scalable programmes, due diligence and measurable outcomes.

Solutions: Co-design, implementation, M1/M2/M3 monitoring and reporting.
