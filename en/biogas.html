Turning organic waste into energy

Organic waste, including animal manure, can be converted through biodigesters into biogas for cooking and biofertilizer for agricultural use.

How it works

  1. Organic material and water enter the biodigester.
  2. Anaerobic digestion produces biogas.
  3. Biogas can support clean cooking when pressure, safety and volume are suitable.
  4. Digestate must be handled correctly before agricultural use.
